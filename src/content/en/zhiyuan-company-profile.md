# Zhiyuan Robotics (AgiBot) — The Volume Leader in Embodied Intelligence

## Company Overview

- **Brand**: AgiBot (Zhiyuan Robotics)
- **Legal Entity**: Zhiyuan Innovation (Shanghai) Technology Co., Ltd.
- **Founded**: February 2023
- **Headquarters**: Pudong, Shanghai, China
- **Founder**: Peng Zhihui ("ZhiHuijun"), former Huawei "Genius Youth"
- **Chairman & CEO**: Deng Taihua
- **Website**: agibot.com

Founded by Peng Zhihui, a former Huawei "Genius Youth" and renowned tech influencer, AgiBot is among China's most prominent embodied intelligence robotics companies. Its mission is "creating unlimited productivity with intelligent machines."

## Core Product Lines

### Yuanzheng (Expedition) Series — Bipedal Humanoids

- **Yuanzheng A1**: First-gen product, 175cm/55kg, 49+ DOF, max speed 7km/h, 200TOPS AI compute. Launched August 2023.
- **Yuanzheng A2**: First mass-commercial full-size humanoid, 169cm/69kg, 40+ DOF. First humanoid with simultaneous China-EU-US certifications (CR, CE-MD, CE-RED, FCC). 2-hour battery, hot-swappable.
- **Yuanzheng A2-W**: Wheeled dual-arm robot, 770×620×1630mm, 230kg, 5-hour battery. Designed for flexible manufacturing.
- **Yuanzheng A2-Max**: Heavy-load humanoid, 175cm/85kg, 67 DOF (53 active), 450Nm joint torque, 40kg payload capacity.
- **Yuanzheng A3**: Next-gen full-size humanoid launched February 2026. The 10,000th unit was an A3.
- **Spirit G2**: 50-DOF wheeled robot, sub-mm precision, 5kg arm payload, 24/7 operation. Launched October 2025.

### Lingxi (Sprite) Series

- **Lingxi X1**: Open-source humanoid robot, 130cm/≤33kg, 31/41 DOF. Fully open-sourced in October 2024 (1.2GB+ of drawings and code).
- **Lingxi X2**: Full-intelligence robot with 31 DOF, emotion engine, touch interaction. Three versions: X2, X2 Pro, X2 Ultra.

### Quadruped D1 Series
- **D1 Ultra**: Industrial-grade, 3.7m/s max speed, 35cm jump, IP-rated.
- **D1 Pro / D1 Edu**: For entertainment and education, launched August 2025 on JD.com.

### Other Products
- **PureDust C5**: Commercial cleaning robot, 1980m²/h, 3-hour battery.
- **AIDEA**: Embodied data system including collection robots, remote operation, and data platform.

## Business Performance

- 2024: ¥100M+ revenue
- 2025: ¥1.05B+ revenue (20x YoY growth)
- Mar 2026: 10,000 units cumulative production (industry first)
- Patents: 317 filed (233 invention) as of June 2025
- 2026 HK IPO planned (target $5.1-6.4B valuation)

## Competitive Edge

1. **Volume Manufacturing**: Only company to reach 10,000-unit production
2. **Open-Source Ecosystem**: Lingxi X1, AimRT framework, GO-1 model fully open-sourced
3. **Brand Power**: Founder Peng Zhihui's strong personal brand
4. **Full Product Matrix**: Bipedal, wheeled, quadruped, and cleaning robots
5. **Global Expansion**: Entered Germany and Malaysia; LG Electronics strategic investment
