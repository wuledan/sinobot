### 1. 📐 The 'Standards Era' for Humanoid Robots: 7 National Test-Method Standards Launched in Optics Valley, 200+ Delegates from Unitree, Xiaomi Robotics, Deep Robotics and Beyond

**In one sentence**: _On Aug 13-14, the launch meeting and first plenary discussion for the 'Humanoid Robot Test Methods' national-standard series took place at the Hubei Humanoid Robotics Innovation Center — seven national standards covering general principles, environmental perception, decision & planning, motion control, manipulation, localization & navigation, and human-robot interaction started drafting in parallel, and the Hubei Humanoid Robot & Embodied Intelligence Standards Working Group was inaugurated with 29 founding member organizations including the Hubei Humanoid Robotics Innovation Center, the Hubei Institute of Measurement and Testing Technology, Wuhan University and Huazhong University of Science and Technology._

> 💡 **Why it matters**: This is the first time China has systematically filled the industry's test-and-evaluation gap through the 'test methods' entry point. The industry's defining pain points over recent years: **divergent technical routes, inconsistent test metrics, incompatible software/hardware interfaces, and a missing common verification framework**. Paper specs often diverge from real-world performance, leaving downstream integrators with no basis for comparison and inflating costs across the R&D, pilot and mass-production chain. The seven standards span the full performance-evaluation spectrum of humanoid robots — from environmental perception to decision & planning, motion control to manipulation, and localization & navigation to human-robot interaction — and, in essence, unify the industry's 'common technical language': **products become testable, data becomes interoperable, and technology becomes benchmarkable**. For the industry, this is institutional groundwork for moving from 'technology demonstration' to 'scaled deployment'; for the country, setting the standards is the high ground in the global race for embodied-AI influence.

**Key facts**:
- **Seven standards**: general principles, environmental perception, decision & planning, motion control, manipulation, localization & navigation, human-robot interaction — drafting started in parallel
- **Attendance**: 200+ industry delegates; Unitree, Xiaomi Robotics, Magic Atom, ZTE, Deep Robotics, Horizon Robotics, Lingxin Robotics, Galbot and other leading companies
- **Universities**: Wuhan University, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Northwestern Polytechnical University, China Jiliang University, University of Bamberg
- **Working group**: Hubei Humanoid Robot & Embodied Intelligence Standards Working Group inaugurated with 29 founding member organizations
- **Leading drafters**: Chengxin Zhilian (Wuhan) leads the 'localization & navigation' standard (founded 2022 → joined the standards committee 2023 → led a test-method standard 2025); HWK (Huawake) co-drafts 'environmental perception' (flexible e-skin specialist)
- **Test scenarios**: Hubei Humanoid Robotics Innovation Center offers 23 categories of real home, industrial and commercial test scenarios — the largest and richest set in China
- **Process**: a closed loop of 'launch & briefing — scenario validation — industry matching — chapter-by-chapter refinement', with seven parallel deep-dive sessions on Aug 14

> 📌 **Conclusion**: When a product must answer both 'what are the specs' and 'how are they measured', an industry truly becomes comparable, iterable and commercializable. The launch of seven standards means China's humanoid-robot race is shifting from 'battling launch-day spec sheets' to 'battling measured performance within a standard framework'. For the whole supply chain, whoever proves product strength inside the framework first holds the ticket to the next round of consolidation.

### 2. 🏭 ShiYun's First Full-Size Humanoid Robots Roll Off the Line: 174cm RD3 Ultra for Special-Operations Scenarios, JD.com Elderly-Care Scenario Lands

**In one sentence**: _On Aug 13, ShiYun Technology held the roll-off ceremony for its first batch of full-size humanoid robots, the RD3 Ultra, at Tianjin's Hexi District 764 Embodied Intelligence Industrial Theme Park — a 174cm industrial-grade full-size humanoid built for special-industry and high-risk operations, with a JD.com robotics 'elderly-care scenario' announced in parallel._

> 📌 **In one sentence**: This is not a showpiece demo unit — it's a full-size humanoid robot built to industrial standards and tied directly to real scenario orders. With the JD.com 'elderly-care' scenario landing and the first special-operation full-size production line going live, domestic full-size humanoids are moving from 'prototype delivery' to 'production-line delivery'.

**Key facts**:
- **Positioning**: industrial-grade, full-function, full-size humanoid for special-operations scenarios; 174cm standard human form; designed to adapt seamlessly to human work environments and existing tools
- **Core capabilities**: always-on autonomous operation, traversal of diverse complex environments, end-to-end safety control; new body architecture combined with world-leading model capabilities
- **Ecosystem**: JD.com Robotics and ShiYun jointly developed an 'elderly-care scenario' now live in Hexi District, providing a standardized 'embodied intelligence + medical/nursing companionship' solution
- **Showcase**: the 'Fighting Master' robot combat demo debuted in northern China — two full-size robots demonstrated dynamic balance and impact-resistance control, replicating punches and sidesteps, then rebalancing instantly after being hit

> 📌 **Conclusion**: From 'running fast' at the E-Town half marathon to 'standing firm' in the combat ring to 'working reliably' in elderly-care settings, the capability verification of full-size humanoids is expanding on multiple fronts. ShiYun's case shows that 'dirty, dangerous, dull and difficult' scenarios — special operations and caregiving — are becoming the beachhead for scaled deployment of domestic full-size humanoids.

### 4. 🏅 World Humanoid Robot Games One Week Out: Floor-Exercise Teams 3→18, Routine Library Expands to 8 Categories with 20+ Moves

**In one sentence**: _The 2nd World Humanoid Robot Games will run Aug 22-26 at the National Speed Skating Oval ('Ice Ribbon'), featuring 51 events and 1,301 matches. On Aug 14, Ling Hui, technical delegate for floor exercise and an international-level gymnastics judge, revealed: floor-exercise entries have grown from 3 teams to 18, and the rules have expanded from 9 individual moves in the first edition to 8 categories with 20+ moves, adding static, support and somersault families — with front and side somersaults joining the original back somersault._

> 📌 **In one sentence**: 'The waist is a robot's weak point — completing a somersault in midair is a genuine challenge.' The floor-exercise event's leap in both scale and difficulty epitomizes this edition's logic of 'standards set through competition': competition rules are evolving into practical technical acceptance standards.

**Key facts**:
- **Scale**: Aug 22-26, Ice Ribbon; 51 events, 1,301 matches
- **Lineup**: 666 teams with 2,056 robots from 16 countries; team count up 138% and robots roughly quadrupled versus the first edition
- **Floor-exercise upgrade**: teams 3→18; routine library from 9 single moves to 8 categories with 20+ moves; new static, support and somersault families; front and side somersaults added
- **New high-intensity events**: long jump, weightlifting, tug-of-war, table tennis — direct stress tests for joint motors and reducers
- **Scenario events**: spanning industry, hospitality, home and logistics; dedicated dexterous-hand events (assembly feeding, housekeeping, fire rescue, book sorting, powder weighing, chopstick bean-grabbing, bottle-cap opening)
- **Standards through competition**: organizers say 'win a medal, take an order; leave the arena, enter the site' — a significant portion of competition rules will evolve into practical technical acceptance standards

> 📌 **Conclusion**: From a half-marathon time of 2:40:42 down to 50:26, and from 9 moves to 20+, one year of the World Humanoid Robot Games captures the real speed of China's embodied-intelligence technology climb. The 'humanoid Olympics' opening in a week will be a concentrated stress test of domestic joint motors, reducers, dexterous hands and motion-control algorithms.
