> 💡 **Why it matters**: These numbers drag the sector back from buzz to delivery. Just as important, China's 97%+ share means the center of gravity for humanoid manufacturing, supply-chain integration and early scenario deployment is now very clearly in China. For investors, this changes the valuation framework: it is no longer enough to ask who raised more money or posted the best demo clip. The harder question is who is deploying faster into factories, showrooms, campuses and service environments.

**Key facts**:
- **Global H1 total**: about **19,100 humanoid robots**
- **China share**: **97%+** of global shipments
- **Zhiyuan**: **8,400 units**, **44%** global share
