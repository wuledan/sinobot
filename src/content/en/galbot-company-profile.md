# Galbot (Galaxy General Robotics) — A Rising Force in Embodied Intelligence

## Company Overview

- **Full Name**: Beijing Galaxy General Robot Co., Ltd. (GALBOT)
- **Founded**: May 19, 2023
- **Headquarters**: Haidian District, Beijing, China
- **Legal Representative**: Yao Tengzhou
- **Chairman**: Guo Xiaoliang
- **Founders**: Yao Tengzhou, Wang He
- **Website**: galbot.com
- **Company Type**: Joint-stock company (foreign-invested, unlisted)

Galbot is an innovative enterprise dedicated to embodied multimodal large-model general-purpose robots. In less than three years since its founding, the company has raised over 6 billion RMB (approximately $800M+), making it one of the best-funded embodied AI startups in China.

## Core Product Lines

### Galbot G1 (Wheeled Dual-arm Humanoid Robot)
Galbot's first-generation embodied large-model robot, officially unveiled in June 2024, notably appeared on stage during NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang's keynote. The G1 features a wheeled chassis with dual-arm design, standing 1.73m tall with an operational range of 0.8m to 2.4m. It achieves sub-0.5mm six-DOF precision, supports voice interaction and natural language understanding, and is powered by a billion-scale operation data-trained embodied model.

### GraspVLA Embodied Large Model
Launched in January 2025, the world's first end-to-end embodied large model pre-trained on billion-level simulated synthetic motion data.

### TrackVLA Embodied FSD Model
Released in June 2025, the world's first production-grade end-to-end embodied FSD large model.

### Galaxy Capsule
An unmanned retail space operated by Galbot robots, unveiled in August 2025, supporting voice ordering, payment, precise grasping, and item delivery — fully autonomous with no remote operation.

### Smart Retail Solution
The world's first humanoid robot smart retail solution, deployable in 50sqm unmanned stores handling 5,000+ product SKUs. In April 2026, Galbot partnered with FamilyMart convenience stores for regular operations at Beijing's Zhongguancun store.

## Competitive Positioning

Compared to Unitree Robotics and Zhiyuan Robotics, Galbot differentiates through:

1. **Wheeled + Dual-arm Architecture**: Prioritizes stability and cost-efficiency for commercial retail scenarios over bipedal walking.
2. **Simulation-Data Driven**: The GraspVLA model is pre-trained on billion-level simulation data.
3. **Commercial Traction**: Partnerships with FamilyMart, Bosch, CATL, and Boda Seiko.
4. **Strong Investor Backing**: CATL, Meituan, SAIC, Sinopec, and national AI funds.

## Industry Outlook

**Opportunity**: Embodied intelligence is a trillion-dollar market. Galbot's early deployment in retail, industrial, and medical scenarios provides a valuable data moat.

**Risks**: The wheeled form factor may limit terrain adaptability; commercial revenue still needs validation; competition from Unitree (IPO-bound) and Zhiyuan intensifies.

**Watch Points**: Depth of CATL/Bosch partnerships; overseas expansion; margin trends; IPO timeline.
